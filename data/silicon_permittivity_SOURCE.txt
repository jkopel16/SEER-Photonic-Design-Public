Crystalline silicon (c-Si) optical constants
=============================================

Source:  M. A. Green, "Self-consistent optical parameters of intrinsic
         silicon at 300 K including temperature coefficients,"
         Solar Energy Materials and Solar Cells 92, 1305-1310 (2008).
         DOI: 10.1016/j.solmat.2008.06.009

Retrieved from refractiveindex.info (database licensed CC0 1.0, public domain).

Original file layout: wavelength in micrometres, with n and k in two stacked
two-column blocks. Here merged into a single table and wavelength converted
to nanometres.

Temperature: 300 K.  k is band-to-band absorption.

Columns in silicon_permittivity.csv:
    wavelength_nm  -- wavelength in nanometres
    n              -- real part of the refractive index
    k              -- extinction coefficient (imaginary part)

Tabulated range: 250-1450 nm, 121 points.
