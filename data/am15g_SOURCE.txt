AM1.5G reference solar spectrum -- Global tilt
==============================================

Source:  ASTM G-173-03 Reference Spectra, derived from SMARTS v2.9.2.
         NREL file: astmg173.xls, "Global tilt" column.
         https://www.nrel.gov/grid/solar-resource/spectra-am1.5

The "Global tilt" spectrum is the standard AM1.5G total (global, hemispherical)
spectral irradiance on a 37-degree sun-facing tilted surface, for the standard
atmospheric conditions (air mass 1.5, ~48 deg solar zenith).

Conversion applied when building am15g.csv:
    photon_flux(lambda) = irradiance(lambda) * lambda / (h c)
This converts spectral irradiance (energy per wavelength) into spectral photon
flux (photon count per wavelength), the correct weighting for photocurrent,
since one absorbed photon yields at most one electron regardless of its energy.
    h = 6.62607015e-34 J s,  c = 2.99792458e8 m/s.

The absolute scale of photon_flux is arbitrary for the enhancement factor E
(a ratio of two spectrally weighted integrals, so any common constant cancels),
but the physically correct scaling in photons m^-2 s^-1 nm^-1 is retained here.

Columns in am15g.csv:
    wavelength_nm       -- wavelength (nm)
    irradiance_W_m2_nm  -- spectral irradiance, Global tilt (W m^-2 nm^-1)
    photon_flux         -- spectral photon flux (photons m^-2 s^-1 nm^-1)

Range trimmed to 280-1200 nm (c-Si PV window with margin), 1041 points.
